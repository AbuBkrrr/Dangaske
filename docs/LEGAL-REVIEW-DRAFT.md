# Legal review briefing — Dangaske Real Estate platform (DRAFT)

**This is a briefing for a Nigerian lawyer, not legal advice.** It was written from general knowledge, not from
checking current statutes; every legal reference below must be verified by your lawyer. Items marked ⚠ are the
ones most likely to stop or change the launch.

## 1. The business (facts for the lawyer)
- Operator: Dangaske Real Estate, RC 8511141, Office B09, Sadiq City Plaza, Rijiyar Zaki by Ring Road, Kano.
- Website/app: marketplace for sale, rent and land listings in Nigeria (starting Kano), at dangaskerealestate.com.ng and an Android app.
- Members sign up by phone number; codes are sent by WhatsApp (and email if provided).
- Money the platform collects or holds: (a) fee to view full listing details; (b) inspection fee (credited to the price if the buyer buys through the platform); (c) escrow payments; (d) easy-buy instalments; (e) SPV unit purchases; (f) title-check fee.
- Data held: names, phone numbers, emails, government ID images (for verification), payment references, messages between members, documents uploaded to a vault.

## 2. Issues to resolve, most serious first
| # | Feature | Concern | Question for the lawyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ⚠ | **SPV investment** | Selling units in a property vehicle to the public is likely a regulated investment / collective investment scheme (SEC Nigeria, Investments and Securities Act). | Is licensing, registration or an exemption needed? What offer documents, investor limits and disclosures are required? Until answered, keep offers in "draft". |
| 2 ⚠ | **"Escrow"** | The platform receives buyer money into its own payment-provider account, then pays out manually. Holding client funds may need a licensed escrow/trust arrangement; calling it "escrow" may mislead if funds are not in a trust or escrow account. | Do we need a bank escrow/trust account or licensed escrow agent? Or should it be renamed ("protected payment") with clear terms? Who bears loss if the platform fails? |
| 3 ⚠ | **Anti-money-laundering** | Real-estate businesses are generally designated non-financial businesses that must register with SCUML and keep KYC/transaction records; large cash-like transactions may need reporting. | Confirm SCUML registration, record-keeping period, reporting thresholds, and whether our ID check meets the standard. |
| 4 ⚠ | **Personal data (NDPA 2023)** | We hold ID images and phone numbers; use WhatsApp and Google Translate (data goes to third parties). | NDPC registration as a data controller; lawful basis and consent wording; data-processing agreements with Meta, Paystack, hosting and email providers; breach-notification duty; retention periods; cross-border transfer rules. |
| 5 | **Estate-agency licensing** | Estate agents are regulated (estate surveyors and valuers body); agents listing on the platform may also need to be registered. | Must Dangaske and its listing agents hold ESVARBON registration? Should we require it and display it on profiles? |
| 6 | **Easy-buy instalments** | A deposit plus monthly instalments with a price mark-up could be seen as credit or an instalment sale. | Does the mark-up make this regulated credit? What must the plan agreement say about missed payments, default, repossession/refund and stamp duty? |
| 7 | **Fees and refunds** | Viewing and inspection fees are charged before any sale. | Consumer-protection disclosure and refund rules (FCCPC). Wording of the inspection-fee credit and when it is refundable. |
| 8 | **"Verified" badges and title checks** | Calling a listing "verified" or issuing a title report creates liability if wrong. | Define exactly what is verified, add disclaimers, and consider professional indemnity insurance. |
| 9 | **Land law** | Transfers of land are subject to the Land Use Act (governor's consent), state registries and stamp duties; the platform only advertises. | Confirm the platform is not acting as conveyancer, and add warnings to use a solicitor. |
| 10 | **Reviews, messages, reports** | Defamation and moderation duties; we store private chats. | Take-down procedure, moderation rights, lawful-access requests, retention. |
| 11 | **WhatsApp / marketing** | Messaging users requires consent; Meta's business and commerce policies apply; push notifications are marketing. | Consent capture, opt-out wording, compliance with Meta's policy (some property/financial content has extra restrictions). |
| 12 | **Taxes** | Fees and commissions. | VAT and withholding-tax treatment; invoicing and receipts. |
| 13 | **Agents' and developers' terms** | Listers warrant title and accuracy; indemnities. | Separate lister agreement: authority to market, commission, indemnity, takedown on fraud. |
| 14 | **Diaspora users** | Visitors from the UK/EU/US bring their own consumer and data law. | Whether GDPR/UK GDPR or others apply; currency display disclaimers (prices are shown in naira; other currencies are estimates). |

## 3. What the site's current Terms and Privacy pages cover
They are short starting templates covering: accounts, listings, fees, escrow, instalments, investment offers, liability and Nigerian governing law;
and for privacy: data collected, use, sharing, translation, retention and rights.
**Gaps to fill:** lister agreement; refund and cancellation policy (inspection and viewing fees); detailed escrow terms (who holds funds, release and
dispute process, timelines, fees); easy-buy plan agreement; investor risk disclosure and SPV documents; acceptable-use and moderation policy;
cookie/storage notice; complaints procedure; contact for data-protection requests (a named officer).

## 4. Documents a lawyer will probably need to prepare
1. Terms of use (rewritten) and Privacy policy. 2. Lister/agent agreement. 3. Escrow agreement/terms.
4. Easy-buy instalment agreement. 5. SPV offer memorandum, subscription form and risk disclosure (if offering).
6. Data-processing agreements with service providers. 7. Internal AML/KYC policy and SCUML filings. 8. Refund policy.

## 5. Suggested order
1. Register/confirm SCUML and NDPC status and agency licensing. 2. Decide escrow structure (or rename). 3. Finalise Terms, Privacy, refund policy and lister agreement.
4. Launch the marketplace, fees and easy-buy. 5. Launch SPV offers only after item 1 in section 2 is cleared.

## 6. Questions for the lawyer to answer in writing
- Which of the registrations above does Dangaske already hold, and which are still needed?
- May the platform hold buyer funds, and under what structure and wording?
- May we sell SPV units to the public or only to a limited group of qualified investors?
- What exact wording should replace the "verified", "escrow-ready" and "fraud protection" claims?
- Which clauses in the current Terms and Privacy pages must change?
